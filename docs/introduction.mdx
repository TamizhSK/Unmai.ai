## Platform Overview

**Unmai.ai** leverages Google Cloud's comprehensive AI ecosystem to deliver enterprise-grade content verification solutions. Our platform integrates advanced machine learning with scalable cloud infrastructure to identify misinformation, authenticate media, and ensure digital content integrity across multiple formats.

## Core Capabilities

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Primary Verification Suite" icon="shield-check">
    - **Fact-Checking Engine:** Cross-reference claims against verified knowledge graphs.
    - **Deepfake Detection:** AI-powered analysis to identify synthetic media.
    - **Credibility Scoring:** Real-time trust assessment.
    - **Safety Assessment:** Automated detection of harmful content.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Advanced Analytics Suite" icon="chart-mixed">
    - **Source Verification:** Domain authentication and origin traceability.
    - **Educational Insights:** Pattern recognition and learning modules.

    - **Global Language Support:** Multi-regional content processing.
  </Card>
